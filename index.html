RECOVERY GENESIS MUTUAL RESPONSIBILITY COMPLIANCE STATUTES:

1. Commitment to Progression: The client acknowledges that physical adaptation, metabolic healing, and musculoskeletal tissue repair require systematic loaded progression and compliance with customized advice.

4. Communication Integrity: Timely video submissions and adherence to check-in schedules form the foundation of our remote intervention model. Adjustments are impossible without data.

5. Respect for the Process: Healing is non-linear. Setbacks in stroke recovery, post-operative mobility, or high-performance prep are part of the physiological adaptation cycle. Patience is mandatory.
